AlbumSafe

A small private Android app that backs up phone photo albums to your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Shows the photo and video albums on your phone; you choose which ones to back up.
  • Uploads them to your own Google Drive, in a folder you choose, and checks every file byte for byte against the copy on Drive.
  • Can back up every night around midnight on Wi-Fi.
  • Can free up space on your phone by moving albums that are fully backed up to the phone's 30-day trash, after checking Google Drive again. Nothing on Google Drive is ever deleted.

Who it is for

AlbumSafe is a personal family project. It is not sold or offered to the public.
